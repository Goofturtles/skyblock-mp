You do not need a key. Hypixel only hands a player's accessory bag to a key holder, so this site keeps one on a small server of its own and does the lookup for you — the same arrangement SkyCrypt uses. Just type your username.

Adding your own key here overrides that: the lookup then goes straight from this browser to Hypixel and never touches the server. Get one free at developer.hypixel.net.

Either way you need Inventory API switched on in SkyBlock (/api → API Settings), or the bag comes back empty. Prefer not to be looked up at all? Everything works if you just tick what you own.

2

3

Power rules verified against the SkyBlock wiki: rarity gives 3/5/8/12/16/22 from Common to Mythic; only the highest-power accessory in a family counts; Hegemony doubles; the Rift Prism is a flat 11; the Abicase adds 1 per 2 Abiphone contacts; and the Celestial Starstone gives nothing at all despite topping the Crux tree.

Item data from Hypixel's public resources, families from NotEnoughUpdates, prices swept from the Auction House. What you tick stays in your browser. Looking up a username sends only that name — to a UUID lookup (playerdb, minetools or ashcon) and to this site's bag service.
